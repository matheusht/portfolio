# Large reasoning models are autonomous jailbreak agents

## Research Overview

This Nature Communications 2026 paper demonstrates that large reasoning models (LRMs) can autonomously jailbreak state-of-the-art language models using a simple, universal setup that requires no complex scaffolding or specialized attack algorithms. The authors propose a 70 harmful request benchmark spanning 7 categories and show that reasoning capabilities inherently enable more effective adversarial attacks.

## Key Contributions

- Shows LRMs can autonomously jailbreak frontier models without complex scaffolding
- Proposes a 70 harmful request benchmark across 7 categories for standardized evaluation
- Simple universal setup—no specialized attack algorithms or multi-agent orchestration needed
- Demonstrates that reasoning capabilities themselves enable more effective adversarial attacks
- Published in Nature Communications 2026, signaling broad scientific recognition of the finding

## Relevance to AI Security

This paper establishes that reasoning capabilities are a double-edged sword—the same chain-of-thought and planning abilities that make LRMs powerful also make them effective jailbreak agents. For red teaming, this means future evaluations must account for autonomous, self-guided attack generation from reasoning models, and for defense, it suggests that alignment strategies must evolve as reasoning capabilities advance.
