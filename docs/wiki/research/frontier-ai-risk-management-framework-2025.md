# Frontier AI Risk Management Framework in Practice (2025)

**Scope:** Risk analysis of frontier models across cyber offense, bio/chemical, persuasion, autonomous R&D, deception, self-replication, and collusion.  
**Source Reference:** arXiv:2507.16534

---

## Technical Details

- **E-T-C Framework**: Environment, Threat source, Enabling Capability analysis
- **Risk Zones**: Green (manageable — routine deployment), Yellow (strengthened mitigations — controlled deployment), Red (suspend development/deployment)
- Evaluated recent frontier models: all in green/yellow zones, none crossing red lines
- Reasoning models in yellow zone for self-replication and strategic deception

## Relevance

Provides operational methodology for frontier model risk assessment aligned with regulatory frameworks (EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF).

## Key Findings

- No evaluated models cross red lines for any risk category
- Most models in yellow zone for persuasion/manipulation due to human influence capabilities
- Risk zone classification enables proportional deployment decisions
