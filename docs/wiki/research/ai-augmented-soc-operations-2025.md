# AI-Augmented SOC: A Survey of LLMs and Agents for Security Operations (2025)

**Scope:** Systematic survey of AI/LLM integration across SOC workflows including detection, triage, investigation, and response.  
**Source Reference:** arXiv, 2025

---

## Technical Details

- Capability-maturity model for AI integration across SOC tiers (Tier 1 triage → Tier 3 threat hunting)
- LLM applications: alert enrichment, natural language querying of security data, playbook generation, root cause analysis
- Agent applications: autonomous alert investigation, multi-tool orchestration, automated containment
- Persistent limitations: false positives, adversarial robustness, explainability, data privacy

## Relevance

As SOCs face alert fatigue (average 960 alerts/day, 40% uninvestigated), AI-augmented operations are becoming critical infrastructure for cybersecurity defense.

## Key Findings

- Tier 1 triage automation is most mature; Tier 3 threat hunting remains human-led
- LLMs reduce mean-time-to-respond but introduce new attack surface (prompt injection into SOC tools)
- Trustworthiness, explainability, and safe AI integration are open challenges
