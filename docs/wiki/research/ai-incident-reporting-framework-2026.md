# Designing Incident Reporting Systems for Harms from General-Purpose AI (2026)

**Scope:** Institutional design of AI incident reporting systems for safety- and rights-related events.  
**Source Reference:** arXiv:2511.05914 (AAAI 2026)

---

## Technical Details

- Seven design dimensions: policy goal, actors (submitters/receivers), incident type, risk materialization level, enforcement, anonymity, post-reporting actions
- Nine case studies from safety-critical industries (nuclear, aviation, healthcare)
- Distinguishes mandatory vs. voluntary reporting thresholds
- Addresses near-miss reporting, safety learning, and legal frameworks

## Relevance

As agentic AI systems cause real-world harm, structured incident reporting becomes essential for safety learning and regulatory compliance (EU AI Act Article 62).

## Key Findings

- Current processes insufficient for agent-specific incidents (lack chain-of-thought, browser history, tool access logs)
- Recommends sharing incident information across organizational boundaries
- Legal frameworks for reporter protection need clarification
