Matheus Theodoro's public presence should make him recognizable as an AI security practitioner: his perspective, judgment, trajectory, and original work. Projects provide concrete evidence of that work and belong within a broader personal identity.

## Visibility goals

- Build a durable public record of original investigations, experiments, evaluations, and useful engineering work.

## Writing principles

Follow the [writing guide](../WRITING_GUIDE.md): short declarative sentences, an early claim, plain language, and one main idea per piece.

Start with a concrete artifact, observation, or question. Explain the mechanism with exact nouns, examples, measurements, code, or diagrams when they help. Include the uncertainty or limitation that changes the reader's conclusion.

Keep observations, interpretations, working hypotheses, and predictions distinguishable. A strong take should have a visible basis. Rejected hypotheses, small experiments, corrections, and changes of mind can be valuable parts of the public record.

Add firsthand judgment. When relevant, explain what the agent did, where a person intervened, what surprised him, and what he wants to test next. Natural humor and casualness can coexist with precision about technical claims.

## Public evidence

Link to public or explicitly cleared artifacts, credit contributions accurately, and keep claims within the evidence. Private context, unpublished findings, and third-party material require their own publication permission. A public result should remain understandable when separated from the social post that introduces it.
